Privacy Policy

Personal SMS Assistant

Last updated September 2026 · Operated by Cameron (individual, personal use)

What this is

This is a personal, single-user text-message assistant. It is built and operated by one individual for his own private use and is not offered as a public product, service, or business.

What is collected

Text messages sent to this number are processed to create, look up, update, and remind about personal tasks and reminders. Message content may be sent to a third-party AI model provider to interpret the request and decide what action to take.

Only the unique ID of each inbound message is stored for duplicate-prevention. Message bodies and phone numbers are not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing.

Who can use it

Only one phone number — the owner's own — is configured to use this assistant. Messages from any other number are ignored.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from this number, or HELP for assistance.
