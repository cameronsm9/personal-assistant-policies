Privacy Policy

Personal SMS Assistant

Last updated September 28, 2026 · Operated by Cameron

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect your name, your mobile phone number, and whether you agreed to receive text messages (your SMS opt-in consent, with the date it was given). When you use the service, we process the text messages you send to the assistant so it can create, look up, update, and remind you about your tasks.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate the service: to reply to your messages, store your tasks, send the reminders you ask for, and honor HELP and STOP requests. Message content may be processed by an AI model provider solely to interpret your request; that provider acts as a service provider and does not use it for marketing.

SMS and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or message delivery, is permitted. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your phone number, your messages, or your opt-in consent to anyone, including lead generators.

Opting out

Text STOP to the assistant's number at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See the Terms & Conditions.

Retention and deletion

Your tasks and account information are kept only while you use the service. Email cameronsm7@gmail.com to ask for your data to be deleted.
