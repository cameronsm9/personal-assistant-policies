SMS program

  • Program name: Personal SMS Assistant.
  • Program description: a task and reminder application used by text message. Users text the assistant to create, look up, and update tasks and reminders; the application replies with confirmations, task summaries, and the reminders the user requested. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

  • Cost: message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.
  • Opting out: you can cancel the SMS service at any time. Text STOP to the assistant's number. After you send STOP, you will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further messages. To rejoin, sign up again or text START.
